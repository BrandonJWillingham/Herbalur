## About This Project

Herbalur is a full-stack e-commerce platform developed for **Herbalur**, a natural skincare, haircare, and wellness brand.

The platform was built to modernize the brand's online shopping experience while providing the tools needed to manage products, customers, orders, inventory, shipping, and marketing through a custom administrative dashboard.

The application includes a responsive storefront, product and category pages, shopping cart functionality, secure checkout, customer management, inventory management, shipping integration, email capture, and first-party analytics.

This project was designed as a production-ready commerce platform rather than a traditional portfolio demo, with an emphasis on scalable architecture, responsive UI, real-world payment processing, database management, and business operations.

All product photography featured throughout the platform was shot and edited by me, with limited use of AI-assisted tools.
